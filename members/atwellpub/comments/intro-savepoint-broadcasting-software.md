We built SavePoint for creators, product owners, and communities who want to run a 24/7 radio or video channel on their own schedule and their own hardware.

If you have a library of music or recorded shows, a Discord server or YouTube channel to stream into, and co-hosts who want to go live in their own time slots, SavePoint runs all of it from one schedule. The core station manager is free to start.
