Jockey Radio puts live radio, RTMP broadcasts, and audio files on Minecraft music discs, and the players near the jukebox hear them through Simple Voice Chat. Releases are on GitHub while the Modrinth listing is in review, and the setup guides in the repository walk through the rest. If you run it on your server, the issue tracker is the place to tell us what works and what does not.
