When working with Opus and Fable I notice that both models pile on a massive amount of stream of thought with difficult to read summaries. Summaries should tell a story, and so I built /grok, a term from Robert Heinlein's book Stranger in a Strange Land that means to understand deeply enough to capture the essence of intended meaning. This converts pages of explanation into several narrative driven paragraphs.
