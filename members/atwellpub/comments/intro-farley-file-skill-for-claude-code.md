This is a fun one. Recently, my wife and I enjoyed our 7th year anniversary together by taking a day trip to Disney World and then a short Royal Caribbean cruise. I really enjoy our vacations because I get to read a book (normally science fiction) without any digital distractions. 

This year I read the book "Double Star" by Robert Heinlein, a story about a political body double, where I learned about "Farley Files"; a system of record-keeping for politicians that records facts and experiences about the people they meet on the job.

So after my vacation ended, I was very excited to get home, open Claude Code, and create a command-line /Farley skill to begin managing my very own Farley File just in case one of my body doubles needs a quick study in the near-far future.
