This is a fun one. Recently, my wife and I enjoyed our 7th year anniversary together by taking a day trip to Disney World and then a short Royal Caribbean cruise. I really enjoy our vacations because I get to read a book (normally science fiction) without any digital distractions. 

This year, I read the book "Double Star" by Robert Heinlein and learned about "Farley Files," a system of record-keeping for politicians that records facts and experiences about the people they meet on the road.  This is not all dissimilar to what many of my peers already do when in contracting and sales, keeping handwritten records on Steno pads or Evernote-like software. 

So after my vacation ended, I was very excited to get home, open Claude Code, and create a command-line `/farley` skill to begin managing my own Farley File.  

And who knows, my own body double might just need to review it one day if they are going to carry me forward while I am on another relaxing Royal Caribbean cruise.
