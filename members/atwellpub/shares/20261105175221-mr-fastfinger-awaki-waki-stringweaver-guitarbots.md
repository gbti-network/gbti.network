“Awaki-Waki” opens Mr. Fastfinger’s 2013 *Stringweaver* EP, a four-track instrumental release written, produced, and performed by Finnish guitarist Mika Tyyskä.[^1]

The song was actually composed years earlier for *The Way of the Exploding Guitar* before being rediscovered in Tyyskä’s archives in late 2012. Tyyskä later described *Stringweaver* as a return to the lighter, more colorful atmosphere of his earlier Mr. Fastfinger material.[^2]

The release also crossed into interactive guitar culture: “Awaki-Waki,” “Practice Heart,” and “Nightvisions” were adapted into special GuitarBots levels that players could perform with their own instruments.[^3]
