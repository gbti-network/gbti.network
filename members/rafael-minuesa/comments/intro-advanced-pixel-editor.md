Last century I spent over 20 years as a Graphic Designer and Pre-Press operator before building websites, and one of the first things I noticed was that every WordPress image editor I tried felt like a toy next to what I was used to. 

Advanced Pixel Editor tries to emulate the way Photoshop and Lightroom work, using ImageMagick on the server so the preview you see is the result you get. 

The before and after slider is there because nobody can judge an edit from memory. 

The AI Edit in version 3.8 goes through the provider you already connected to WordPress, so the plugin adds no account and no bill of its own. 

The free plugin is complete on its own. The Pro add-on pays for the time, and 20 percent of its proceeds go to the ImageMagick project, which does the real work. 

Tell me on the support forum what you miss.
