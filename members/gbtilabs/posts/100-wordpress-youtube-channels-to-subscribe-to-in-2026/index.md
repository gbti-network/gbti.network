We've assembled a list of the top 100 WordPress YouTube channels to follow in 2026.

## Who's YouTubing about Wordpress in 2026? Our Selection Process

To be included in our Top 100 list, a channel first had to meet one major rule: it posted at least one video about WordPress within the 2026 calendar year.

In our research, we found 177 channels that met this rule, leaving out hosting companies, which are not part of this study. However, we needed to narrow our list down to meet our 100-channel goal.

To do this, we created an inclusion algorithm that gives every channel a score out of 100, built from the density of WordPress-based videos within the channel, how many it posted in 2026, and how widely its videos are watched. The 100 highest scores make our top 100 list.

### How the score works

Each channel has a score out of 100; #1 has the highest. The score rests on four measures, each ranked against every channel we measured and weighted as shown:

- **WordPress focus (40%):** share of 2026 videos about WordPress
- **Volume (20%):** WordPress videos posted in 2026
- **Reach (20%):** median views per video
- **Engagement (20%):** views compared with subscribers

Subscriber count alone does not raise a score. Plugin and theme companies count at 75%. Five channels are always included whatever their score, which is why the official WordPress channel sits at #100.

## Types of Channels Surveyed

### Who makes WordPress videos in 2026?

The channels vary widely in size. The median channel has **8,425 subscribers**. Fourteen channels have **100,000 subscribers** or more, while 21 have fewer than 1,000. Together, the 100 posted **4,046 videos about WordPress in 2026**.

### What is the topic density related to WordPress for these channels?

Across all 100 channels, 4,046 of the 6,097 videos posted in 2026 are about WordPress. That is 66% of total videos posted. So this list ended up representing channels that trend to creating videos about or involving WordPress.

![Share of the videos posted in 2026 that are about WordPress, by channel type. All 100 channels: 4,046 of 6,097 videos (66%). ### AI is in one title in five

Of the 6,097 titles posted by the 100 channels in 2026, 1,190 use an AI term. That is 20%, or one title in five. We found this interesting, but AI mentions did not affect the final scores in any way.
