Hi everyone,

At the time there is no call to action. I just wanted to share my thoughts, predictions, and maybe potentially inspire the market. If you’ve made it this far, you’re a champion at paying a attention and you have my gratitude.

Regarding the prediction paper:

I have a high degree of confidence that the material laid out in this projection is where the world is headed. I know a lot of you agree with me. We have all been riffing off the same ideas for months now. 

As a product developer I wonder, could I build this? You bet. However, these ideas are not unique and from a proprietary view point I imagine that there are agencies who are building similar management systems right now.  

It makes sense too; the people who spearhead the development of these new management systems are already managing dozens (or hundreds of clients). They can afford the effort and it serves their survival. They theoretically should be the leaders of the effort (and most likely they are). 

Will they develop this to remain proprietary or do they have intentions to productize it? I do not know. My gut tells me that this is where a paper like this might inspire someone to take their efforts to the next level. And if this is you reading this now, you should. Anything worth building well should have the consideration of being also turned into a product. 

Will someone larger, someone already within the managed product space (hosting or CMS) step up and make the CMS/Hosting agnostic infrastructure and make it open to all? They should. They would be first movers, they would ensure that their interests were directly integrated into the vendor options, they could corner the adoption. 

Building it well is still not the same as winning the market.

Will we see many iterations of these systems in the meantime? Maybe not in 2026, but in 2027 the crystal ball says, “absolutely”. 

It is early enough for clear winners.
