“Cynnes” is a 2023 track from French electronic duo il:lo, built around piano, soft vocal textures, deep-house rhythms, and a resonant atmosphere the duo compared to hearing music inside a subway tunnel.[^1]

The track arrived during the run-up to their *Myriad* EP, where il:lo described it as reconnecting with elements of their earlier sound while incorporating influences from a darker, more dance-oriented period in Berlin.[^2] *Myriad* ultimately placed “Cynnes” alongside tracks including “Soldiner,” “Shifted,” and “Ink.”[^3]

The duo has since moved into another chapter with *Lucent*, their debut full-length album, released in October 2026.
