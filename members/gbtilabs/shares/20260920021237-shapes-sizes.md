**Ivri**, born **Ivriana Wright**, is a Florida-born, Brooklyn-based singer-songwriter and producer whose music blends bedroom pop, shoegaze, indie rock, and psychedelia. She first gained attention online as a teenager and has since developed into a distinctive alternative artist.¹

The linked song, **“shapes&sizes,”** comes from her 2022 EP *Sometimes* and captures the softer, lo-fi side of her early sound. Her later work, including **“tower of memories,”** would evolve into a TikTok sensation leading into a listener adoption that is still growing today.  

September 2026, Ivri and her talented band remain an upcoming and underrated American artist.
