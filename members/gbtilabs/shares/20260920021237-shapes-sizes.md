**ivri**, born **Ivriana Wright**, is a Florida-born, Brooklyn-based singer-songwriter and producer whose music blends bedroom pop, shoegaze, indie rock, and psychedelia. She first gained attention online as a teenager and has since developed into a distinctive alternative artist.¹

The linked song, **“shapes&sizes,”** comes from her 2022 EP *sometimes* and captures the softer, lo-fi side of her early sound. Her later work, including **“tower of memories,”** shows how that style evolved into something bigger and heavier.¹
