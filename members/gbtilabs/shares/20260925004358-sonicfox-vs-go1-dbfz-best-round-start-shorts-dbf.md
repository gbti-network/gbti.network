This clip comes from *GO1 vs. SonicFox* at the *Dragon Ball FighterZ Masters Showdown*, part of Dragon Ball Games Battle Hour 2026 in Los Angeles. The two players have a long competitive history in *Dragon Ball FighterZ*, including major tournament meetings at EVO in 2018 and 2019.¹

The synchronized opening shown in the Short was intentional rather than an accidental sequence of perfectly matched inputs. GO1 and SonicFox used Base Vegeta to perform a rehearsed series of attacks, counters, and clashes before transitioning into the actual match. GO1 went on to win the set 2-0.²

The moment works as both a technical performance and a reference to one of *Dragon Ball FighterZ’s* best-known competitive rivalries, which helps explain why the clip circulated well beyond the usual fighting-game audience.
