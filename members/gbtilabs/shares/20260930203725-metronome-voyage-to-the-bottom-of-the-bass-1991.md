*Voyage to the Bottom of the Bass* was released by Metronome in 1991 on WBR Records, catalog number WBR 101 R. The four-track release contains two remixes of the title track, the original mix, and “Straight Jacket,” and is generally cataloged across electronic, breakbeat, techno, and ambient styles.¹ 

The track also appears in documented DJ selections from the period, including a 1991 Carl Cox set, and has continued to turn up in later mixes from DJs including Eris Drew and John Tejada.² ³
