Gigi D’Agostino’s **“L’Amour Toujours”** was released in 1999 and became one of the defining European dance tracks of its era. Its title translates roughly to **“love always,”** and beneath the electronic production is a simple, emotional melody that adapts surprisingly well to pipe organ.

This organ version was performed by **Sven H. BPM** at **St. Laurentius Church in Lichtenstein, Saxony, Germany**, in connection with a 2020 confirmation service. Played on the church’s historic Jehmlich pipe organ, the Eurodance melody takes on the character of a hymn, giving the song a much more solemn and reflective tone.
