A large collection of Terence McKenna’s recorded and written work is being turned into a new audio and video podcast. *Blue Streak*, hosted by his daughter Klea McKenna and produced by Lux Natura with SpectreVision Radio, launches November 10, 2026.[^1]

The series reaches beyond the lectures most listeners already know. Planned material includes restored radio conversations, informal recordings with friends and fellow thinkers, field tapes from ethnobotanical work in the Amazon, and dramatized readings from McKenna’s journals and letters.[^1]

There is a substantial archive behind it. Lux Natura says it has gathered nearly 60 boxes of material, including roughly 600 audio cassettes alongside letters, journals, photographs, video, books, and other objects, with hundreds of tapes already digitized.[^2]

That makes *Blue Streak* less like a conventional retrospective and more like an ongoing excavation of material that has not always been easy to access. The podcast page offers an early look at what the family and archivists are preparing, and is worth visiting before the series begins.
