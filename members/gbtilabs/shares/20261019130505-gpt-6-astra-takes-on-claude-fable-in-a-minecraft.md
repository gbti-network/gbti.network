A Minecraft creator connected GPT-6 Astra and Claude Fable to a custom building system and asked them to generate structures directly inside the game. The tests quickly escalate from fighter-jet runways and blacksmith houses to submarines, cities, a Star Destroyer, an entire planet, and builds generated from reference images.[^1]

In this particular experiment, Astra consistently produced the creator’s preferred results, especially as the requested builds became larger and more detailed. Fable remained capable, but also tended to consume substantially more tokens, forcing the creator to impose tighter limits during some rounds.[^1]

The image-input portion may be the most interesting part, with the models translating photographs and illustrations into surprisingly recognizable block structures. Still, this is a creator demo rather than a controlled benchmark. Other head-to-head build tests have produced much more mixed results between Astra and Fable depending on the task and environment.[^2]

The video keeps pushing the system further as it goes, and the later builds are worth seeing rather than describing. Watch the original if you want to see just how large these generated Minecraft structures become.
