Age of Enlightenment is a mix by Canadian drum & bass DJ Allan Hendricks, performing as DMS. Mixcloud categorizes the set under ambient and atmospheric drum & bass and lists music from Debarge, Bravil, Tim Cant, Soulstructure, Drum Origins, Seathasky, and others. The mix also contains spoken-word samples from Alan Watts, Herbie Hancock, and the film Dinner with Andre.
