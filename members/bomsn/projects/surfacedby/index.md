## SurfacedBy

SurfacedBy tracks how AI assistants talk about a brand. It asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Mode the questions a brand's buyers ask, with Claude, Grok and DeepSeek available as add-ons, and records which brands each answer names, which pages it cites, and where a competitor gets the recommendation instead.

The result is one number from 0 to 100, the AI Visibility Score, backed by the real answers behind it. A Daily Check catches movement between runs, and a weekly Full Check covers every platform on the plan.

## From measurement to action

Beyond the dashboard, SurfacedBy drafts the work that closes a gap, and nothing ships until someone approves it:

- **Content.** Research-backed briefs and drafts aimed at the citation gaps, with one-click publishing to WordPress or Shopify.
- **Community replies.** It watches the Reddit, X and LinkedIn conversations that shape answers in a market and drafts a reply in the brand's voice for the ones worth answering. Every reply is edited and approved by a person, and the drafts learn from those edits.
- **Publisher outreach.** It finds the sources AI cites that do not cite the brand yet, and drafts a pitch for each.

After something is published, SurfacedBy re-checks the same prompts and ties any change in visibility, citations, traffic or conversions back to the action.

## What it tracks

- **Tracked prompts.** The buyer questions are discovered automatically, with no prompt lists to write, and each one can target a specific market (the US, the UK, Germany, France, Brazil, Japan and six more).
- **Citations and sources.** Every page AI cites, classified by type: Reddit threads, review sites, best-of listicles, documentation.
- **Competitors.** Found from real AI answers, each with a summary of its strengths, weaknesses and openings.
- **Brand themes with sentiment.** How AI describes the brand on themes such as price, support, speed and quality.
- **AI content readiness.** Each Full Check audits up to 250 of the brand's pages for how easily AI can understand and cite them.
- **AI traffic and revenue.** Visits sent by AI assistants, tied to signups and sales, installed with a WordPress plugin, a line of code or a Cloudflare Worker. Google Search Console and Google Analytics connect alongside.

## For teams and developers

Workspaces carry owner, admin, editor and viewer roles, and agencies keep each client in its own workspace with its own billing. Visibility data is available through a REST API on the Professional and Agency plans, through pay-as-you-go checks in the SurfacedBy Console, and through an MCP server on every plan, so an AI assistant can ask what changed and what to fix.
