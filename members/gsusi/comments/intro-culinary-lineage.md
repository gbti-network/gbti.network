Most family recipes were never really written down. They live in how someone cooks: "the little blue cup, nearly full", "until the sizzle softens". I built Culinary Lineage so that person can just talk, and it only asks about what is missing, so the book ends up in their words without anyone filling in the gaps. Start with the dish everyone asks for; the first recipe is free.
