Sondeas is for people who dictate between appointments. Record what you are allowed to record while it is still fresh, or upload an existing file, and you get back a punctuated transcript split by speaker that you can rewrite into a draft, translate into any of 100 languages or turn back into speech. You review the draft; the official record stays in your approved clinical system. It runs in Chrome, Edge and Safari on desktop and mobile, with nothing to install. Your content sits behind a passphrase you set, rotate and revoke, so we cannot read it. There is a Clinician Plan at €29 a month for regular use, or pay-as-you-go minutes from $2 if you only need it now and then.
