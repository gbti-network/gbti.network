MadTicket — Making event ticketing simpler for everyone.

MadTicket is a Spain-based event ticketing platform I built to make discovering, managing and selling tickets easier for both event organisers and attendees.

For attendees, it offers event discovery by city, secure payments with Stripe, Apple Pay support, QR code tickets and Apple Wallet integration.

For organisers, MadTicket provides everything needed to manage ticket sales, including multiple ticket types, discount codes, affiliate programmes, real-time analytics and financial reporting, with no monthly subscription fees.

The platform also includes two dedicated mobile apps:

* MadTicket Backoffice — Manage events, track sales and revenue, and monitor performance in real time.
* MadTicket Scan — Validate tickets at the entrance, manage check-ins and prevent duplicate entries.

On the technical side, MadTicket is built with Next.js, React, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Prisma and Stripe. It also uses AI-powered multilingual semantic search with pgvector, allowing users to discover events across different languages.

What started as a solution for my own events has grown into a live SaaS product used by over 1,000 users.

I’m continuing to develop and expand the platform, with the goal of making professional event management accessible to organisers of all sizes.
