Devote is a daily devotional app I built for Windows, macOS and Android to help people who struggle to focus. To keep things manageable and distraction-free, it opens each day to a short sequence: a moment of quiet, the day's reading with Matthew Henry's commentary, and a few reflection questions. Thank you for taking a look.
